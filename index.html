Loosen examples

Every way to wire Loosen into a search page, on one real catalogue: 186 apartments around Lausanne. Open a variant, type filters that cannot match, and the widget explains which filters conflict and what to relax, one click each.

Guide: loosen.sicena.com/docs/install · Server: /docs/server · Data: data/README.md

This is the live demo of the loosen-examples repository: the same pages you get with ./serve.sh --with-server, plus a real loosend behind /api/ for variant 8. Fork or clone the repository to run them on your machine.

What you will see

Search rent ≤ 500 CHF, minutes to the centre ≤ 5, in Lausanne or Pully, with a balcony and a view: no result. The panel under your filters says (this is the real output of page 5):

Any of these changes gives results:
  [Rent ≤ 706 CHF · 1 result]   [Clear Features · 6 results]   [Minutes to the centre ≤ 9 min + Clear Town · 1 result]
  Why?
    No results with Rent ≤ 500 CHF and Minutes to the centre ≤ 5 min and Features: balcony and view
      [Rent ≤ 550 CHF]  [Minutes to the centre ≤ 9 min]  [Clear Features]
    No results with Rent ≤ 500 CHF and Town is Lausanne or Pully and Features: balcony and view
      [Rent ≤ 550 CHF]  [Clear Town]  [Clear Features]

Each chip applies the change to your filters (fields, state object, URL), your list refreshes, and an Undo button appears. Two searches with no result in this catalogue: rent ≤ 400 + not furnished + metro M1 and rent ≤ 600 + centre ≤ 5 min + parking; every page has buttons for them.

The variants

  • 1Client mode, ES module

    mount from vendor/loosen.mjs. Data as an array, a URL or an async function.

    OpenGuide 2, 4

  • 2Classic <script>

    window.Loosen from the IIFE build, for sites without modules.

    OpenGuide 2

  • 3Plain form: fromForm

    The thesis form as ordinary HTML (numbers, towns, yes/no radios) and a live result table.

    OpenGuide 5.1

  • 4By hand

    Your own markup: explicit getConstraints, applyRelaxation, restoreConstraints, onPreview.

    OpenGuide 5.2

  • 5Facet state object

    An Algolia / Typesense / Meilisearch style UI: checkbox facets and range sliders around one state object.

    OpenGuide 5.3

  • 6React 18

    A real React app (vendored UMD, no build, no JSX): useLoosen with a ref and an effect. Tested.

    OpenGuide 5.4

  • 7Server-rendered shop needs ./serve.sh --with-server

    Filters in the URL, results rendered by a Go program (stdlib only), relaxations navigate to the new URL. Back is the Undo.

    OpenGuide 5.5

  • 8Server mode: loosend needs ./serve.sh --with-server

    The analysis runs on your Loosen server; read key, CORS, catalogue loaded with curl. Docker, binary or compose.

    OpenServer docs

  • 9Options playground

    Toggle every option live, watch the event stream, theme with CSS custom properties, override texts, keyboard notes.

    OpenGuide 9
